Our story

A Labor of Love, Woodsmoke & Edo Heritage

Jollof Spot started as a backyard feast in GRA, Benin City, grown from a grandmother's cast-iron pot. Real party jollof can't be rushed on an electric cooker, so we still cook ours over firewood, the slow way, until the bottom pot catches just right.

No shortcuts, no synthetic seasonings, and never compromising on the firewood smoke. Every guest is treated like family at a Sunday celebration.
